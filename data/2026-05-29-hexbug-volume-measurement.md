## Measurement of Hexbug Displaced Volume

## Objective

The purpose of this experiment was to determine the displaced volume of a Hexbug.

This volume measurement will be used later to calculate how much flotation material is needed to make the Hexbug neutrally buoyant in mineral oil.

Two methods were used:

1. Direct volume measurement using a graduated cylinder
2. Indirect volume measurement using Archimedes' principle and a digital scale

---

## Background

According to Archimedes' principle, the buoyant force on a submerged object is equal to the weight of the displaced fluid.

For water:

- Density of water ≈ 1.00 g/mL

Because the density of water is approximately 1.00 g/mL, the increase in scale reading in grams is approximately equal to the displaced volume in mL.

---

## Method 1: Graduated Cylinder

### Procedure

1. Filled a graduated cylinder with water.
2. Recorded the initial water level.
3. Completely submerged the Hexbug in the water.
4. Recorded the final water level.
5. Calculated displaced volume.

Formula:

> Volume Displaced = Final Reading - Initial Reading

### Data

| Trial | First Reading (mL) | Final Reading (mL) | Volume Displaced (mL) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 110 | 115 | 5 |
| 2 | 84 | 90 | 6 |
| 3 | 96 | 102 | 6 |
| 4 | 106 | 112 | 6 |
| 5 | 104 | 110 | 6 |

### Result

Average displaced volume:

> 5.8 ± 2 mL

The uncertainty was large because it was difficult to read the graduated cylinder precisely.

---

## Method 2: Buoyant Force Measurement Using Scale

### Procedure

1. Filled a beaker with water and placed it on a digital scale.
2. Tared the scale.
3. Suspended the Hexbug above the beaker using a string.
4. Lowered the Hexbug completely into the water without allowing it to touch the bottom or sides.
5. Recorded the increase in scale reading.
6. Since water has a density of approximately 1.00 g/mL, the scale increase in grams was interpreted as displaced volume in mL.

### Data

| Trial | Volume Displaced (mL) |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 5.673 |
| 2 | 5.823 |
| 3 | 5.450 |
| 4 | 5.715 |

### Result

Average displaced volume:

> 5.665 ± 0.001 mL

---

## Comparison of Methods

| Method | Average Displaced Volume (mL) |
|---|---:|
| Graduated Cylinder | 5.8 |
| Scale / Archimedes Method | 5.665 |

Difference:

> 5.800 - 5.665 = 0.135 mL

Percent difference:

> (0.135 / 5.8) × 100 ≈ 2.3%

The two methods produced very similar results, differing by approximately 2.3%.

---

## Discussion

Both methods gave consistent measurements of the Hexbug's displaced volume.

The graduated cylinder method produced a slightly larger average volume, but the difference was small compared to the measured volume.

The scale method was more precise, so we will use this value for future neutral buoyancy calculations:

> Hexbug displaced volume ≈ 5.665 mL

Since 1 mL = 1 cm³:

> Hexbug volume ≈ 5.665 cm³

---

## Sources of Uncertainty

### Graduated Cylinder Method

- Difficulty reading the water level precisely
- Parallax error
- Small air bubbles attached to the Hexbug
- Water sticking to the Hexbug during insertion and removal

### Scale Method

- Scale resolution and measurement uncertainty
- Movement of the suspended Hexbug
- Incomplete submersion of small features
- Air bubbles attached to the Hexbug
- Small oscillations of the string
