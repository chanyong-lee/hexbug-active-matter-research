### Flagella Material

We discussed what material should be used for the flagella-like part of the microrobot.

In biology, flagella appear thin, flexible, and thread-like.  
For our prototype, we need to decide whether the flagella should be:

- A flexible material that bends during motion
- A more solid structure that keeps its shape
- A hybrid design with both flexibility and stability

This decision matters because the material will affect how the robot moves through fluid.

---

### Access Hole for Electronics

We discussed adding an access hole in the microrobot design.

The access hole should allow:

- Connection to the microcontroller
- Programming access
- Charging access
- Plugging in cables without taking the whole robot apart

This is important for making the prototype easier to test, modify, and repair.
